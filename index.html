Vocabulario legal / Legal vocabulary

Ever — Alguna vez
At any time, unless the question specifies otherwise.

Convicted — Condenado
Found guilty of an offense.

Detained — Detenido
Held and not free to leave.

Cited — Citado
Issued a citation for an alleged violation.

Charged — Acusado
Formally accused of an offense.

Plead guilty — Declararse culpable
Admit guilt in a legal proceeding.

Probation — Libertad vigilada
Court-ordered supervision under conditions.

Parole — Libertad condicional
Conditional release; immigration parole has a different meaning.

Removal — Remoción
Formal immigration process or action to remove a person.

Deported — Deportado
Removed from a country under immigration law.

Allegiance — Lealtad
Loyalty to a country.

Oath — Juramento
A solemn promise.

Bear arms — Portar armas
Carry weapons.

Noncombatant — No combatiente
Service not involving fighting.

Overdue — Vencido
Not paid by the due date.

Misrepresentation — Tergiversación
A false or misleading representation.

Lawful permanent resident — Residente permanente legal
A person granted permanent resident status.

Selective Service — Servicio Selectivo
U.S. registration system for potential military conscription.

Alimony — Pensión conyugal
Financial support for a spouse or former spouse.

Dependents — Dependientes
People who rely on another person for support.
